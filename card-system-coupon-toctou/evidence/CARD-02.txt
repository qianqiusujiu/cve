=== Interpretation ===
1. A single-use coupon (count_all=1) was applied to THREE concurrent orders.
2. All three orders have discount=1, paid=0 (100% off) and status=2 (STATUS_SUCCESS).
3. Three DISTINCT cards were attached and shipped for free (card_order rows below).
4. Note on count_used: Eloquent persists the stale in-memory counter (0+1=1) with an absolute-value UPDATE, so the counter converges to 1 and the ledger looks clean afterward; the actual double-spend is visible in the orders/shipments (3 free orders from a 1-use coupon).
5. Root cause: Pay::buy() pre-checks the coupon with a non-locking SELECT (whereRaw count_used<count_all) OUTSIDE the order transaction; consumption (status=USED; count_used++) happens later inside DB::transaction with no SELECT...FOR UPDATE on the coupon row.
